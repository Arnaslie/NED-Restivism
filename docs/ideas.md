## Humane design research

Summary from the brainstorm that led to [decision 0006](decisions/0006-humane-design-patterns.md). Citations are from well-known work and still need checking before public use.

- **If-then plans** (Gollwitzer): deciding "when X, I'll do Y" in advance reliably raises follow-through.
- **Social norms** (Cialdini): people copy what they believe their group does.
- **Self-determination theory** (Deci & Ryan): lasting change comes from autonomy, competence and belonging; rewards and pressure crowd it out.
- **Behaviour = motivation × ability × prompt** (Fogg): make rest easy and time the prompt well.
- **Fresh-start effect** (Dai, Milkman & Riis): people change more readily at new beginnings.
- **Stopping cues**: natural endpoints help people put the phone down (the opposite of infinite scroll).
- **Activist burnout** (Chen & Gorski, 2015): martyrdom culture and guilt about rest are central, so the team is the unit of change.
- **"Rest is resistance"** (Tricia Hersey): frames rest as political, not selfish. Check with the activist before using the phrase.
- Later: leaders resting visibly first (needs a way that doesn't add leader labels).
